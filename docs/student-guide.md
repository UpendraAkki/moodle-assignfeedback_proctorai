# What students see

*Teachers: you can share this page with your class.*

Your teacher uses **Proctor AI** to help mark this assignment. The AI reads your answers and suggests marks. **Your teacher checks every mark** before you see it, and can change anything.

## Handing in your work

You hand in the assignment in Moodle as usual. Depending on what your teacher allows:

- **Photos** of your handwritten pages (JPG, PNG, HEIC from an iPhone, or WebP);
- **one PDF scan** of your pages;
- **typed answers** in the online text box.

### Tips for photos the AI can read well

- Take one photo per page, in good light, straight from above.
- Make sure the whole page is in the photo and nothing is cut off.
- Avoid shadows across the writing, and blur. Hold still, or rest your elbows on the desk.
- Write each question's number next to your answer, the same way the questions are numbered.
- Upload the pages **in order**, for example `page1.jpg`, `page2.jpg`.
- At most **20 pages** in total. Each photo can be up to 10 MB, and a PDF up to 30 MB.
- Word documents, PowerPoint and other files **can't be read** by the AI. Your teacher will see that and mark them by hand, but it may take longer.

## When will I see my marks?

Not straight away. The AI's suggestion goes to your teacher first. You see your marks and feedback **when your teacher releases them**, as with any other assignment.

When they are released you will see, under **Feedback** on the assignment page:

- your grade;
- your teacher's feedback;
- **Marks per question**: your mark for each question and a short comment.

## Changing your work after handing it in

If your teacher allows you to edit your submission, you can still change it. If it hadn't been graded yet, the new version is the one that counts. If it had already been graded, your teacher decides whether to grade it again.

## Your privacy

- Your **name, email and student number are not sent** to Proctor AI, only your pages and typed answers.
- If you write your name on the paper, the AI can see it on the photo, but the plugin doesn't keep it.
- Proctor AI deletes the uploaded pages within 72 hours.

Questions about your marks? Ask your teacher, just as you normally would.
